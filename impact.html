Strategic Reflection: 2021-2025 Lessons

Our transition to the 2026-2028 plan is guided by solid historical insights.

Core Achievements

  • Expanded care & treatment sites from 45 to 70+
  • Antenatal testing coverage reached 85% state-wide
  • Established strong grassroots CBO and civil society networks

Key Challenges

  • High reliance on donor funding (80%+ of budget)
  • Stigma preventing youth and men from seeking services
  • Supply chain vulnerabilities for diagnostics and ARVs

Emerging Opportunities

  • Scaling digital health dashboards & Electronic Medical Records
  • Establishing the state HIV Trust Fund for sustainable financing
  • Leveraging diaspora partnerships and private CSR frameworks

Major Intervention

Governor's Wife Rallies Support for Ifechukwu at Regina Caeli Hospital

Following reports of the worsening health condition of Ifechukwu, a young girl battling Advanced HIV Disease (AHD), Her Excellency, Chief Dr. Mrs. Nonye Soludo, immediately intervened through the Healthy Living Initiative.

This prompt response reflects the Soludo administration's people-centered commitment to protecting vulnerable citizens and ensuring no one is left behind in the fight for a healthy Anambra.
