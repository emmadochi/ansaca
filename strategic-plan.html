A Message from the Executive Director
"Anambra stands at a pivotal juncture. With steady decline in new HIV infections, improved treatment coverage and strengthened community engagement, we now resolve to innovate, adapt, and leave no one behind on our journey to an AIDS-free State."

The SSTSP 2026–2028 is not merely a policy document — it is our collective covenant with every person living with HIV, every community at risk, and every generation yet unborn. It is anchored in the bold UNAIDS 95-95-95 targets and aligned to the Sustainable Development Goal to end AIDS by 2030.

Implementation Timeline

3-Year Roadmap

From Foundation to Consolidation — a phased approach to an AIDS-Free Anambra.

26

2026

Foundation Phase

Laying the digital and structural groundwork for the entire plan period.

  • Deploy real-time Anambra HIV Dashboard and Electronic Medical Records system
  • Launch KYS-WI (Know Your Status, Win Incentives) quarterly campaigns state-wide
  • Recruit and train 1,693 HIV Awareness Brigade (HAB) volunteers across all 326 wards
  • Commission Moonlight Testing Initiative (MTI) for key populations in Onitsha, Awka
  • Establish toll-free telehealth helpline: 0906 048 6600
27

2027

Expansion Phase

Scaling up programs and securing legislative and fiscal milestones.

  • Achieve 20% reduction in new HIV infections from 2023 baseline
  • Scale PMTCT antenatal coverage to 92% across all health facilities
  • Pass Anambra State Anti-Stigma and Non-Discrimination Bill through State House of Assembly
  • Make Anambra State HIV Trust Fund operational with first disbursements
  • Expand mobile riverine boat clinics to cover all 5 riverine LGAs
28

2028

Consolidation Phase

Achieving bold targets and embedding a self-sustaining response.

  • Achieve 50% reduction in new HIV infections from the 2023 baseline
  • Reach 97% viral suppression rate among PLHIV on treatment
  • Launch Anambra State HIV Research Institute (ASHRI) — a Centre of Excellence
  • Implement workplace accreditation levies targeting ₦150M/year local revenue
  • Achieve 70% reduction in mother-to-child HIV transmission
Sustainable Financing

From Donor Dependency to Local Resilience

The SSTSP 2026–2028 is anchored on a bold transition away from the current 80%+ donor-dependency model. We are building a multi-stream local financing ecosystem that will secure the HIV response beyond 2030.

HIV Trust Fund

A dedicated State HIV Trust Fund seeded by Anambra State Government, with co-financing from domestic banks and philanthropic partners. Target capitalization: ₦500M by 2028.

Workplace Accreditation Levies

Organizations operating in Anambra State will earn HIV-Responsible Workplace certification. Accreditation levies and compliance frameworks are projected to generate approximately ₦150M/year.

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

A formal CSR platform connecting private sector partners (banks, manufacturers, telecoms) to strategic HIV programs through measurable public health ROI frameworks.

Diaspora Partnerships

Structured engagement of the Anambra diaspora community to channel remittances and philanthropic contributions toward targeted health interventions.

Multi-Sectoral Partnerships

Join the SSTSP Movement

The SSTSP 2026–2028 calls on every sector of society — government, private sector, civil society, development partners, academia, and communities — to contribute to an AIDS-Free Anambra.

MDAs & Government

Ministries, Departments and Agencies co-implementing prevention, treatment and policy reforms under the SSTSP framework.

Donor Partners

International and local donors including PEPFAR, NACA, UNAIDS, WHO, GF, bilateral agencies, and foundations supporting catalytic investments.

CSOs & Communities

Civil Society Organizations, Faith-Based Organizations, and community groups implementing grassroots prevention, care, and advocacy programs.

Researchers & Academia

Universities, research institutes, and public health scientists contributing evidence, models, and innovations to the Anambra HIV response.
