Our Services

Comprehensive Care & Support

We provide a wide range of services designed to educate, protect, and support the communities of Anambra State.

Prevention & Epidemic Control

Quarterly KYS-WI Campaigns

State-wide 'Know Your Status, Win Incentives' (KYS-WI) mobile campaigns reaching markets and parks.

Learn More

Moonlight Testing Clinics

Night-time testing clinics tailored for key populations and high-risk environments.

Learn More

Permanent Testing Booths

Fixed, walk-in testing and counseling booths strategically located in high-traffic commercial hubs.

Learn More

Youth School Clubs

HIV awareness clubs in secondary and tertiary institutions to educate and empower students.

Learn More

Toll-Free Telehealth Helpline

Confidential, 24/7 tele-counseling, support and referral services via 0906 048 6600.

Call Now: 0906 048 6600

Treatment, Care & Support

Mobile Riverine Clinics

Dedicated boat outreach clinics bringing prevention and treatment to hard-to-reach riverine communities.

Learn More

PrEP & PEP Scale-Up

Widespread distribution and clinical support for Pre-Exposure and Post-Exposure Prophylaxis at care facilities.

Learn More

Universal PMTCT Scaling

Eliminating mother-to-child transmission through early diagnosis and antiretroviral treatment for pregnant mothers.

Learn More

PLHIV Socio-Economic Empowerment

Vocational training and seed grants to support financial independence and combat stigma for people living with HIV.

Learn More
