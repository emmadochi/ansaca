About ANSACA

Leading the fight for public health.

The Anambra State AIDS Control Agency (ANSACA) is dedicated to coordinating the state's response to the HIV/AIDS epidemic. We work tirelessly to ensure equitable access to prevention, treatment, and support services for all citizens.

Our Mission

To implement comprehensive, multi-sectoral strategies that halt and reverse the spread of HIV in Anambra State.

Our Vision

A healthy Anambra State where new HIV infections are eliminated and those infected live healthy, productive lives.
